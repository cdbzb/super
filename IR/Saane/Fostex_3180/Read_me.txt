Fostex 3180 Spring Reverb

Captured by Fokke van Saane
Thanks to Pluk for lending me his unit. 

It was hard not to overload the springs. Even at very low inputs i could hear square waves, so i had to lower the inputs a lot. As a result the output of the unit became so low i had to use a mic preamp with approx. 50 dB gain to get a decent level in my AD converters. Of course a  huge noise was the result.  Beside the noise the unit became very sensitive. If i clapped my hands i could hear the springs going. So i used a 300 seconds sweep to get the best SN ratio. 

The great thing about this IR related to the real unit is that the noise is gone. I still hear a slight abrubt ending of the tail, i think that is where the reverb falls into the noise. This is the best i could do, with the knowledge i have now.  In use, i think this tail cutoff won't be a major problem. At least i hope so!

I hope you make nice mixes with it. This IR is for free, but you owe me a copy if you make a nice cd with it. Please send me at least an mp3!
