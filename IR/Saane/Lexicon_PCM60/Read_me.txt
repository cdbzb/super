To all users of the PCM60 IR

I captured all room and plate settings of this unit in the 16 combinations possible with Size and Time. I left alone the bass and treble contour buttons. If the list will be too long for you, just remove the settings you don't like.

In the list you will find two letters; S and T, with a number, wich refers to Size and Time. 1 is small or short, where 4 is large or long. There is a pic in it explaing this too.

I have no intention that this IR will sound exactly the same as the real unit, but i tried as much as possible to come as close as possible.

Thanks to Frank van der Weij for lending me his PCM60.
